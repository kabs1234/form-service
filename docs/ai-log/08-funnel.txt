● The undefined is the locale argument. Both Intl.NumberFormat and
  Intl.ListFormat take the locale first ('en-US', 'de-DE' and so on), and
  passing undefined means "use the viewer's browser language." So a German
  browser would show 50 % and "A und B", while every other word on the page
  stays English. formatSubmittedAt in response-table.ts:55
  (src/lib/response-table.ts#L55) already does the same thing.

  I'd replace it with a fixed APP_LOCALE = 'en-US' constant in src/lib, because
  the UI copy is English and numbers, dates and lists should match it. I'd use
  it here and in formatSubmittedAt, so no undefined is left anywhere. The
  alternative is navigator.language, which keeps the per-browser behaviour
  without the undefined, but gives the same mixed-language result.
